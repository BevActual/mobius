# A tour of the workspace

Every app in Mobius has the same frame, so once you know one you know them all.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left belongs to the app you are in: Notes shows your notebooks, Mail your mailboxes, Browser your
tabs. Its **head** is the app switcher. Click it and the sidebar turns into the list of your apps; pick one and it turns
back into that app's sidebar.

- Right-click an app in the list to move or hide it.
- An app Mobius is working in glows while it works. An app it changed while you were elsewhere carries a dot until you
  look.
- A narrow window folds the sidebar to a rail of icons.
- The **New** button at the top follows the app: New note, New task, New tab, New event.

## Mobius in every app

The **Mobius** button turns the sidebar into the conversation with Mobius, right where you are working. Mobius sees the
app you have open (the note, the page, the selected text) and can work across all your apps from there.

- **Pin beside** keeps the conversation open next to the page instead of in the sidebar.
- **Little Mobius** (Ctrl+Shift+M) is the conversation in a small window that stays above your other apps.
- The ask box in an app's title row ("Ask about this note") starts a message about what is open.

## The desk

The Agent app opens on the **desk**. It shows what waits for you first (questions and approvals you can answer in
place), then what Mobius is doing, what is open in your apps, and what is scheduled next. The sidebar here lists your
conversations, with tabs for **Sessions**, **Activity** and **Agents**.

## Finding things

- **The launcher** (Ctrl+K) is one search for apps, open pages and commands. "Ask Mobius" is always its first row.
- **The app hub** shows every app as a tile with what is happening in it, grouped by what it is for.
- **Search** looks through everything Mobius keeps: conversations, notes, tasks and pages.

## Split view

Ctrl+\ shows two apps side by side. Drag the line between them to resize, or double-click it to put it back in the
middle.

## Changes you review

Whatever Mobius changes is drawn **dashed** until you decide. Each app has the same review bar with **Keep**,
**Discard** and **Try another**. [Working with Mobius](../guide/working-with-mobius.md) explains how this works.

## Comments

Press C, or the speech-bubble button, to comment on anything in an app. Send a comment to Mobius and its answer comes
back in the thread, or keep it as a note for people. See [Review](../apps/review.md#comments).

## Settings

Ctrl+, opens Settings. Its search box finds any setting by what it does ("dark", "fallback", "dictation").
