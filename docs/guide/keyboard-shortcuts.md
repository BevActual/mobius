# Keyboard shortcuts

Change any of these in **Settings › Keyboard**: click a shortcut's key field and press the new keys (Esc cancels).
The small x clears a shortcut, and **Reset** puts back the usual keys.

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Ctrl+K | Find apps, pages and commands (the launcher) |
| Ctrl+Space | The launcher from any app, even when Mobius is in the background |
| Ctrl+, | Open Settings |
| Ctrl+Shift+\ | Show two apps side by side, or one |
| Ctrl+Shift+L | Open or close Little Mobius |
| Ctrl+Shift+Space | Dictate in the message box: hold and let go to stop, or tap to start and tap again to stop |
| Shift+Tab (in the message box) | Next mode: Agent, Ask, Plan, then the app's own mode |
| 1 to 4 (in the Mobius panel) | Pick one of the Actions, or answer a question by its number |
| Ctrl+L | Go to the address bar (Browser) |
| Ctrl+T | Open a new tab (Browser) |
| Ctrl+W | Close the current tab (Browser) |
| Ctrl+F | Find on the page |
| C | Comment mode: comment on anything in the app |
| Esc | Stop reading a reply aloud; close a menu or dialog |

Mobius keeps a few keys for itself, the same in every app; no shortcut can take them, and Settings › Keyboard lists
them: Ctrl+Shift+M shows and hides Mobius beside the work, Ctrl+\\, Ctrl+Alt+\\ and Ctrl+J show and hide the panels on
the left, right and bottom, and Ctrl+N makes something new in the open app. (Little Mobius was Ctrl+Shift+M; a saved
Ctrl+Shift+M moves to Ctrl+Shift+L.)

**Launcher from anywhere** (Ctrl+Space) opens the launcher in its own small window over whatever app you are in. Pick
something and Mobius comes forward with it open; press Esc or click elsewhere and you are back where you were. In Mobius,
Ctrl+Space opens the launcher there, like Ctrl+K. Change or turn off its key in **Settings › Keyboard**.

**Quick entry from anywhere** brings Mobius up and starts a message even when Mobius is in the background. It has no
key until you set one.

Each app also has its own keys, listed in its menus. A few worth knowing:

- **Slides:** Ctrl+Enter plays from the picked slide. While playing: arrows, Space or a click go on; B blacks the screen; P shows the presenter view; Esc stops.
- **Design:** V, F, R, O and T pick the move, frame, rectangle, oval and text tools.
- **Code:** Ctrl+/ comments lines, Ctrl+G goes to a line, Tab and Shift+Tab indent.
