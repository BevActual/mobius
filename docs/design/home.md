# Home: one Inbox

The Home round of 25 November 2026 (one app at a time). The app list Andrew decided on 22 November
(`/mnt/project-files/design/apps-equivalents.md`) puts "one list of what needs you across apps" in Home, and folds the
Review app into it. The second design pass's asks for Home (chats newest first, the suggestions above the composer,
In your apps on one line) were already in. Linear's Inbox is the reference: one place, each row says what it wants
and can be answered there.

## The Inbox

At the top of the desk, under the greeting and Get started, with its count beside the heading. It holds, in this order:

- **Chats that need you**: a question, or an approval with **Allow** in the row (was under Now).
- **Changed while you were away**: each app Mobius changed while you were elsewhere, "Changed by Mobius while you were away". A press
  opens the app; the row goes once you have looked, as the dashed edge did. These were a dashed edge on the app's row in In your apps; they are said once now.
- **Suggested replies**: replies Mobius drafted in Mail, one row with the count; a press opens Mail's suggested replies.
- **No reply yet**: mail sent with Remind if no reply whose day has come with no answer. A press opens the sent
  message; **Stop waiting** lets it go.

The line under the greeting counts the Inbox ("3 waiting for you"). With nothing waiting there is no Inbox at all, and
the desk ends "That's everything for today." as before.

## Working

What Mobius is doing, with Stop, under its own heading after the Inbox: it does not wait on the person, so it is not in
the Inbox.
