# Code of conduct

Mobius follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with people who are new, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work, not the
person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else
the project lives.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@BevActual](https://github.com/BevActual), privately on GitHub. Reports
are kept confidential.
