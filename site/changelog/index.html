Version 0.1.26 24 November 2026

The new look

  • Files moves to the new look: rows that float without lines, a quiet grey mark where Mobius changed something, and only places in the Places panel.
  • Docs, Notes and Calendar get the last touches of the new look.
  • The Mobius panel starts closed, so each app opens with room for your work.

Settings

  • Accounts lists each account once, with the apps it's used for, and a page per account to switch apps on or off, sign in again or remove it. Contacts accounts can be added too.
  • Back and Forward move between the pages you've opened, and Escape keeps what you're typing.
  • Every page sits in one readable column, and opens where you asked: the setting a search found, or the page's top.
  • The arrow keys move through search results.

Music

  • Freeze now covers Session clips as well as the arrangement.
  • Auto Pan-Tremolo is a new effect, for sound that moves between left and right or pulses in level.
  • Chorus-Ensemble has its full controls in the window: Chorus or Vibrato, Feedback, Invert and a high-pass filter.
  • An audio clip's pitch can change over time, with a Transposition envelope in the Detail panel.
  • Beat Repeat's Insert mode no longer clicks when the repeats start and stop.

Fixes

  • The View menu's ticks show what is really on.
