How it works

One quote. Five apps. No copy and paste.

A supplier sends a quote. Follow it through your workspace: Mobius takes it where it belongs, and every change waits, dashed, for you to keep.

You stay in charge

It suggests.
You decide.

Anything Mobius makes shows up in place with a dashed edge. Keep it, discard it, or ask for another take. Every step is recorded, so you can rewind any conversation.

  • Sending, paying, deleting and running commands always ask, whatever mode you pick.
  • Choose how far it goes: Ask each time, Review changes, Apply changes or Auto.
  • No hidden memory. What Mobius knows about you is on pages you can read and edit.

Made for making

Not just paperwork.

Write a song, build a deck, sketch a screen. Mobius works here too: in Music it writes notes, never audio, so a bassline it adds is yours to edit like any other part.

Bring your own AI

Your plan, your keys, or your own computer.

Mobius isn't tied to one AI company. Sign in with a plan you already pay for, add a key, or run a model on your own graphics card, and pick a different model for each job.

  • ChatGPT planOpenAI's sign-in for open-source apps

  • GitHub CopilotThrough GitHub's Copilot CLI
  • OpenRouterSign in on OpenRouter's own page
  • API keysOpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, xAI, Azure, Bedrock and more
  • On your computerBuilt-in llama.cpp, or Ollama and LM Studio

Local and open

Yours, on your computer.

Your notes, documents, sheets and settings are real files you can open in any other app. There is no Mobius account and no Mobius server, and the code is MIT licensed on GitHub.
