Mobius Workspace

Every app. One agent.

Mobius Workspace is an AI workspace for your computer: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Mail, Docs, Files, a browser and more in one app, with an agent that works across all of them.

Yours, on your computer

Mobius is a desktop app. Your notes, documents, tasks, mail and settings are files on your own computer. It works with services you already use, such as your mail account, your calendar server and your cloud drive, and it signs in to them directly. There is no Mobius account and no Mobius server.

Bring your own AI

You choose the model service Mobius talks to, or run a model on your own computer. Changes Mobius makes show as drafts until you keep them.

Open source

Mobius is free and open source under the MIT licence. The code, releases and documentation are on GitHub.
