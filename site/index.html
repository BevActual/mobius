How it works

One quote. Five apps. No copy and paste.

A supplier sends a quote. Follow it through your workspace: Mobius takes it where it belongs, and every change waits, dashed, for you to keep.

You stay in charge

It suggests.
You decide.

Anything Mobius makes shows up in place with a dashed edge. Keep it, discard it, or ask for another take. Every step is recorded, so you can rewind any conversation.

  • Sending, paying, deleting and running commands always ask, whatever mode you pick.
  • Choose how far it goes: Ask each time, Review changes, Apply changes or Auto.
  • No hidden memory. What Mobius knows about you is on pages you can read and edit.

Every app

23 apps. One look. One agent.

Start with the everyday six and add the rest from Store when you want them. Apps load the first time you open them, so the ones you don't use cost nothing.

Assistant

  • HomeYour desk and your conversations with Mobius.
  • AgentsHelpers that run the jobs you repeat, on a schedule.
  • StoreAdd apps, connectors, skills and plugins.
  • SettingsPick which AI does which job, and how far it goes.

Write and plan

  • NotesQuick pages, saved as Markdown files.
  • DocsLonger documents with an outline, comments and history.
  • TasksLists of what's due, planned with Mobius.
  • CalendarYour week at a glance, and time found for you.
  • SheetsSpreadsheets with formulas, saved as CSV.
  • DatabaseSQLite tables you can question in plain words.
  • ProjectsOne piece of work: its files, rules and memory.
  • KnowledgeWhat Mobius knows, on pages you can edit.

Browse and find

  • MailYour inbox, sorted, with drafts in your tone.
  • MessagesSlack, Matrix, Teams and Telegram in one list.
  • BrowserA full browser Mobius can read and compare in.
  • FilesYour whole workspace as a drive of real files.

Create

  • SlidesDecks with layouts, presenter notes and a player.
  • DesignFrames, layers, components and smart guides.
  • PhotoCrops, adjustments and filters you can undo.
  • MusicA studio on Mobius's own audio engine, in Rust.
  • VideoCut footage into a short film on a timeline.
  • CodeAn editor with git, and changes you review line by line.
  • CanvasA whiteboard of stickies, shapes and arrows.

Made for making

Not just paperwork.

Write a song, build a deck, sketch a screen. Mobius works here too: in Music it writes notes, never audio, so a bassline it adds is yours to edit like any other part.

Bring your own AI

Your plan, your keys, or your own computer.

Mobius isn't tied to one AI company. Sign in with a plan you already pay for, add a key, or run a model on your own graphics card, and pick a different model for each job.

  • ChatGPT planOpenAI's sign-in for open-source apps
  • Claude planThrough Claude Code on your computer
  • GitHub CopilotThrough GitHub's Copilot CLI
  • OpenRouterSign in on OpenRouter's own page
  • API keysOpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Mistral, xAI, Azure, Bedrock and more
  • On your computerBuilt-in llama.cpp, or Ollama and LM Studio

Local and open

Yours, on your computer.

Real files you own

Notes, documents, sheets and settings are files on your computer. Open them in any other app, any time.

No Mobius account

There is no Mobius server. Mobius signs in straight to the services you already use, and keys stay in your system's secret store.

Free and open source

MIT licensed and built in the open. Read the code, file an issue, or send a fix on GitHub.
