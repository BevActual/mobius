Mobius

Privacy policy

Effective [DATE]. This policy covers the Mobius desktop app, published by [PUBLISHER NAME].

The short version

Mobius is an app that runs on your computer. What you keep in it stays on your computer and in the services you connect it to. We do not run a Mobius server and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your data. The only things that leave your computer are requests Mobius makes for you: to the AI model service you chose, to services you connected (such as your mail, calendar or cloud drive), and to GitHub to check for updates.

Where your data goes

WhatWhere it goes
Your notes, documents, tasks, conversations, memory and settingsFiles in the Mobius data folder on your computer.
What you ask Mobius, and what it reads to answerTo the AI model service you chose in Settings, under that service's own terms. With a model that runs on your computer, nowhere.
Passwords, API keys and sign-in tokensThe secret store of your operating system (Windows Credential Manager). Each one is only ever sent to the service it belongs to.
Mail, calendars, contacts and files from your accountsCopied between that service and your computer, directly. Never through a server of ours.
Update checksMobius asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and downloads it from there. GitHub sees the request as it would any download.

Mobius has no analytics, advertising or tracking. Email is shown without scripts or remote images, so tracking pixels in messages do not load.

Google accounts

If you choose to sign in with Google, Mobius asks for access to the Google services you connect, such as Google Drive (and, later, Google Calendar). You see exactly what it asks for on Google's sign-in screen before you allow it.

  • What Mobius accesses. The files in your Google Drive, and later your calendar events, so it can show them in Mobius, keep a copy on your computer in step with Google, and make the changes you ask for.
  • How it is used. Only to provide those features to you. When you ask the agent about a file or an event, the parts it needs to answer are sent to the AI model service you chose, as with anything else you ask about.
  • Where it is kept. On your computer. Your Google sign-in token is kept in your operating system's secret store and sent only to Google.
  • Sharing. We do not receive your Google data, so we cannot sell it, share it, use it for advertising or use it to train AI models. Mobius does not use Google data to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Mobius's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account in Mobius at any time, or revoke Mobius's access from your Google account's third-party access page.
  • Settings › Privacy › Remove my data deletes everything Mobius keeps on your computer.
  • Settings › Privacy › Privacy report shows what Mobius read and changed in the last seven days, and where your requests were answered.

Children

Mobius is not directed at children under 13.
