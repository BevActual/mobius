Messages

Your chats in one place, with a catch-up.

Messages is made to bring Slack, Matrix, Teams chats and Telegram into one list, through each service's official API. Signing in to some of them is still on the way.

What you can do

  • Catch me up

    Get at most five lines on what was said and decided in a channel, plus a draft reply to anything waiting on you.

  • One list

    See all your conversations together or one account at a time, with mentions and unread counts.

  • Mobius space

    Your own chat space, run on this computer, that opens straight to its first channel.

  • Threads in place

    Open a thread's replies right where you are, then step back to the channel.

  • Ask about your chats

    Ask Mobius from any app, like "anything urgent in Ops?", and it reads the chats it needs.

With MobiusMobius reads your chats as someone else's words, sums them up and suggests replies, but it has no way to send: only you do.

Where it's keptMessages stay with each chat service and are read as you open them.
