Video

Cut footage into a short film.

Drop in footage, add clips to the cut and trim them on a filmstrip timeline. Export the finished film with its titles and sound.

What you can do

  • Filmstrip timeline

    Each shot is a numbered filmstrip you can trim from either end, split, duplicate and move.

  • Precise trims

    Set where a shot starts and ends as timecode or plain seconds.

  • Full screen viewer

    Play the shots in order, and full screen when you want a proper look.

  • Export the film

    Save the film, titles and sound included, as a video file in the project's Exports folder.

  • Clips stay untouched

    Your original clips are never changed.

With MobiusYou can ask Mobius about your cut.

Where it's keptEach project is a folder in your workspace, with the cut saved as a small file and finished films in an Exports folder.
