Mail

Your mail, sorted, with drafts Mobius writes.

Your mail is mirrored to this computer and sorted into Primary, Transactions and Updates. Mobius suggests replies and plans, but it never sends: you press Send.

What you can do

  • Sorted for you

    Mobius sorts the Inbox into Primary, Transactions and Updates, and you can move any message to another.

  • Suggested replies

    A reply waits under the message as a dashed draft until you choose Keep and edit, Discard or Try another.

  • Take care of this

    One ask turns an email into tasks with due dates, a calendar event and a suggested reply, each waiting for you.

  • Get to zero

    Mobius proposes a plan for the whole inbox, dashed until you keep it.

  • Triage mode

    Sort unread mail into Reply, Later and Archive with replies drafted and nothing sent.

  • Shown safely

    No scripts and nothing loaded from the web, so tracking pixels never fire.

With MobiusMobius sorts, triages and drafts replies, but it never sends mail itself: you press Send.

Where it's keptA copy of each account's recent Inbox and Sent mail, with your drafts, is kept in your workspace.
