Apps/Browse and find

Browser

A full web browser, with Mobius to help.

Tabs live in a side panel, as in Safari and Arc, and a new tab opens on your favourites and reading list. Ask Mobius about the page you are on, or select text and act on it.

What you can do

  • Tabs in a panel

    Pin, reorder and search your tabs and bookmarks in one side panel.

  • Reader mode

    Keep a page in Read later and read it as clean, plain text, picking up where you stopped.

  • Keep highlights

    Highlight passages as you read, ask about them, and copy them all to Notes.

  • A private start page

    Your new tab page is drawn by Mobius, so none of it is shown to a web page.

  • Mobius on the page

    Ask about a page or act on selected text; clicking or typing on a page asks every time.

  • Your sign-ins stay yours

    When a page needs a sign-in, you sign in yourself.

With MobiusMobius reads and works on pages for you, asks before opening a site it has not been allowed, and asks every time before it clicks or types.

Where it's keptPages you save to read later are Markdown files in your workspace.
