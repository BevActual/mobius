Slides

Presentations, played as Keynote plays them.

Edit each slide at 16:9 with presenter notes underneath, then play the deck full screen. Layouts, pictures and transitions are close at hand.

What you can do

  • Presenter view

    See the next slide, your notes and the clock while you present.

  • Layouts and pictures

    Start a slide from a layout, and paste or drop in pictures that sit beside your words.

  • Slide transitions

    Choose how each slide moves on: None, Dissolve, Push or Move in.

  • Skip a slide

    Leave a slide out of Play without deleting it; it stays in the list, dimmed.

  • Presenter notes

    Write notes under each slide and show or hide them as you work.

With MobiusAsk Mobius about the deck and its changes come back as dashed slides, and Keep keeps every slide it changed.

Where it's keptEach deck is a Markdown file in your workspace, with its pictures in a folder beside it.
