Files

Your whole workspace as a drive.

Every note, sheet and document is a real file, and Files shows them all in a list like Finder's. Double-click anything to open it in its own app.

What you can do

  • Made by Mobius

    Everything Mobius created in one place, dashed until you keep it, with a count of what is waiting.

  • Trash with Put Back

    Deleted files go to the Trash, where Put Back returns them to the folder they came from.

  • Drag to move

    Drag files onto any folder to move them, with Undo if you change your mind.

  • Add a drive

    Add a WebDAV server such as Nextcloud or a Synology, or an S3 bucket, and browse it like your workspace.

  • Sync a folder

    Keep a workspace folder in step with a drive both ways, keeping both copies when they clash.

  • Careful saving back

    A changed copy goes back to a drive only if nobody changed the file there since you copied it.

With MobiusA file Mobius made is dashed until you keep it, and Mobius can never empty the Trash.

Where it's keptFiles shows your workspace folder itself; a drive's files stay on its server, and its password sits in your computer's secret store.
