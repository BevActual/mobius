Photo

Your pictures, with edits you can always take back.

Browse your albums as a grid and edit with adjustments, filters and crop. Your original files are never changed.

What you can do

  • Originals untouched

    Edits are kept beside each photo, so every change can be put back.

  • Fine adjustments

    Exposure, highlights, shadows, contrast, saturation, warmth and more, on all of a picture or just part of it.

  • Filters and crop

    Apply a filter, or turn, flip and straighten a picture in Crop.

  • Show original

    Compare with the picture before your edits, or Revert to start over.

  • Recently Deleted

    Deleted photos keep their edits until you empty it, and can be put back in their album.

  • Export a copy

    Export writes a new file next to the photo.

With MobiusPictures Mobius makes with your Images model are saved here.

Where it's keptAlbums are folders in your workspace, with your original files untouched and your edits in a small file beside each one.
