Apps/Write and plan

Calendar

Your calendars by day, week, month or year.

See your time at a glance, with a soft colour for each calendar and a red line for now. Tasks due at a time sit right beside your events.

What you can do

  • Your calendars together

    Use Mobius's own calendars, subscribed calendar links and CalDAV accounts such as iCloud, Fastmail and Nextcloud.

  • Quick events

    Type an event in plain words, like "Lunch fri 1pm", or sweep across the hours to make one.

  • Tasks on the calendar

    Tasks due at a time show at their hour as a thin line you can tick off.

  • Invites and answers

    Invite people with names from your address book, and see who is going, maybe or not going.

  • Record a meeting

    Start recording from an event, and its page in Notes names the event and who is on it.

  • Year view

    See the whole year on one screen, with a dot under each day that has events.

With MobiusEvents Mobius adds or suggests are drawn dashed until you Keep, Discard or Try another, and the Mobius panel tells you how much free time is left today.

Where it's keptYour calendars are .ics files in your workspace that other calendar apps can open.
