Knowledge

What Mobius knows, on pages you can read.

Knowledge opens on your private How I work page, where everything Mobius remembers about you is in plain sight. Shared pages, like each project's memory, sit beside it.

What you can do

  • How I work

    What Mobius remembers, grouped as About you, Preferences, Projects, People and Learned skills.

  • Nothing learned silently

    What Mobius learns waits at the top, dashed, until you Keep it or say Not true.

  • Edit or forget

    Edit, forget or share any line, or add one of your own.

  • Shared pages

    Changes to a shared page are offered, not written straight in, and wait with Decline, Edit first and Keep.

  • Earlier versions

    See what putting an earlier version back would change, line by line, and Undo if you change your mind.

With MobiusMobius offers what it learns as dashed lines on How I work, and you decide whether each one stays.

Where it's keptPages are Markdown files in your workspace, and what Mobius remembers about you is one plain Markdown file in its data folder.
