Apps/Write and plan

Tasks

Lists of things to do, with dates and repeats.

Each task is one clear line, with its due date, flag and subtasks at the edge. Smart lists like Today, Scheduled and Flagged gather what matters from all your lists.

What you can do

  • Smart lists

    Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged and Completed gather tasks from every list, with Scheduled grouped by day.

  • Times in plain words

    Type a time after the day, like "Call Sam tomorrow at 3pm", and the task is set.

  • Repeating tasks

    Repeat every day, weekday, week, month or year; ticking one off moves it to its next day.

  • Details and subtasks

    Each task holds notes, a due date, a flag, its list and its subtasks in a panel beside the list.

  • Lists on a server

    Connect a CalDAV account such as iCloud Reminders, Fastmail or Nextcloud and its lists appear in Tasks.

  • Easy undo

    Deleting a task or a list shows Undo, and a task you just ticked can be clicked back for a moment.

With MobiusTasks Mobius adds are dashed until you keep them, and Discard puts the list back as it was before that turn.

Where it's keptEach list is a Markdown file of checkbox lines in your workspace. Lists from a CalDAV server stay on that server, with a copy in Mobius's data folder.
