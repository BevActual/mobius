Apps/Write and plan

Docs

Longer documents, with an outline and comments.

Write on a clean white page with headings, tables, pictures and links, and move around with the outline. A quiet line under the title says when you last edited it and how many words it has.

What you can do

  • Outline as you go

    Your headings sit under the open document; click one to jump there, and the one you are reading is marked.

  • Comments with answers

    Leave comments beside the page, and Ask Mobius on one to get its answer right in the thread.

  • Writing tools

    Mobius works on a selection or the whole document, showing the words it took out struck through.

  • Review mode

    Have Mobius comment and suggest without touching your text.

  • Earlier versions

    Put an earlier version back, and Undo takes it back again if you change your mind.

  • Share and export

    Copy a document, export it as a web page or Markdown, print it or save it as a PDF.

With MobiusEach change Mobius makes is one dashed block in the page with its own Keep, Discard and Try another, and your open comments go along when you ask it about the document.

Where it's keptEach document is a Markdown file in your workspace.
